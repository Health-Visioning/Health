How far to trust this.
  • The rows aren't independent. Of the 104 still-elevated rows, 9 are whole-chapter rollups, 35 are sub-groups and 60 are individual codes, and they nest (a chapter, one of its sub-groups and a code inside that sub-group can all appear). It is fewer than 104 separate signals.
  • 99 of the 104 are also red on age-standardized rates. The other 5 are red only on raw counts, so population growth and ageing may explain part of them.
  • The families are hypotheses, not tested causes. They come from each row's ICD-10 code and direction of change. Family 1 is weakest for its musculoskeletal rows (arthrosis, spine disorders, osteoporosis): those conditions rarely cause death on their own, so a change in how deaths are certified, in line with the near-quadrupling of ill-defined causes, is at least as likely as deferred care.
  • "Back near trend" is a borderline-sensitive test. It means 2024 sits within two standard deviations of the pre-2020 scatter around the trend line, i.e. it looks like a normal pre-pandemic year. Accidental poisoning (X40-X49) passes only just: 2024 is 1.9 standard deviations above trend (16.6 against 13.1 per 100,000), having fallen from its 2021 peak of 20.2.
  • Family 5 contains none of the still-elevated rows by design. It explains the rows that ran below trend (respiratory disease, suicide, perinatal conditions, viral hepatitis).
