Weekly deaths by cause, smoothed, with the pandemic timeline overlaid

2019 is now included as a pre-pandemic reference year — the variant, vaccine, and restriction lanes stay blank for 2019 since none of that existed yet, but the death-count lines extend back a full year so you can compare each cause's normal pre-pandemic pattern (like the winter cardiovascular peak) against what happened from 2020 onward. This uses the true weekly series (no calendar-month binning) and applies a 5-week centered moving average to smooth out short-term reporting noise while preserving real waves. Click a disease button to toggle it on or off — the y-axis rescales automatically to whatever's currently selected. A dashed "All causes (total)" button is included but starts off, since its much larger scale would otherwise flatten every individual disease line.

Cause categories and weekly counts are Statistics Canada's own "selected grouped causes of death" classification (Table 13-10-0810-01). A 5-week centered moving average is applied to each cause independently before plotting; edge weeks (start/end of series) average over fewer than 5 weeks. Values are provisional and may be revised in later StatCan releases, especially for the most recent weeks shown.
