Nutrition explorer

Pick any two nutrients and see where common foods land.

Click a group to focus on it. Click a dot to see its full nutrition.

Put the foods in order, most to least of a nutrient. Drag a card or use the buttons. You get 3 checks, and correct foods lock in green after each one. Reveal a value if you're stuck; the result shows how many you used.

Values are approximate averages per 100 g, based on typical USDA FoodData Central figures; real foods vary by variety and preparation. % Daily Value uses FDA adult reference values for a 2,000 calorie diet. Sugar and sodium are limits, not targets. This is for exploring, not personal dietary advice.
