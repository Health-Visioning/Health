Chapter IV: Endocrine, nutritional and metabolic diseases — deaths by year and sex

Males

Diabetes mellitus is by far the leading cause, though it has declined every year since 2022, from about 4,394 to about 3,939 deaths.

Females

Diabetes mellitus also leads among females and has declined steadily since 2020, from about 3,355 to about 2,935 deaths.
