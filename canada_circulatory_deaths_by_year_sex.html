Chapter IX: Diseases of the circulatory system — deaths by year and sex

Canada, 2020-2024, by sub-cause [I00-I99]. Click a legend item or a bar segment to isolate that sub-cause across both panels — the y-axis rescales to fit it; click again to show all. Source: Statistics Canada, Canadian Vital Statistics – Death database.

Males

Ischaemic heart disease is by far the leading circulatory cause of death among males, roughly half of all circulatory deaths.

Females

Ischaemic heart disease and other forms of heart disease are close leading causes among females, with cerebrovascular disease also prominent.
Circulatory system deaths, Canada: 38,927–42,109 (males), 35,317–37,290 (females), 2020-2024.
